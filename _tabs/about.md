# Hi, I'm Selahattin Duman

I'm a senior Computer Engineering student at **Ankara University**, quietly obsessed with cybersecurity.

## What I Do

During my university years, I've wandered through web, network, and mobile security — and somewhere along the way, **Application Security** stole my heart. There's something deeply satisfying about understanding how an application breathes, finding the cracks nobody else noticed, and helping fix them before anyone gets hurt. It's a bit like being a locksmith who thinks like a burglar.

That curiosity has also given me the chance to work with multiple companies in the industry, turning classroom theory into real-world practice.

## Why This Blog

I started this little corner of the internet to document my journey and share what I learn along the way. If even one post helps someone taking their first steps into security, that's a win for me. Expect write-ups about:

- Application security concepts and hands-on labs
- CTF challenges and Hack The Box adventures
- Projects I'm building (and breaking, then fixing)
- Honest lessons from the industry
